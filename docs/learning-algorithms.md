# Learning algorithms

First exercise pass earns 10 XP for the stable exercise/mastery revision. First lesson completion earns 20; first self-reported mission completion earns 50. Unique keys prevent repeats. XP never gates navigation.

Attempts retain grading origin and assistance. A solution reveal permanently marks subsequent attempts on the same item/mastery revision. Resetting the editor does not reset evidence.

Demonstration needs two distinct independently passed objective items, including application/debugging/transfer. Self-assessment alone cannot establish it. Retention adds two successful objective due reviews on UTC dates at least seven days apart; overdue retained material becomes review_due. These are product heuristics, not scientific measurements.

Good advances through 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days; Easy advances two steps; Hard keeps the step and waits one day; Again resets to zero and waits ten minutes. Wrong objective responses force Again. Server time is authoritative. Stale revisions conflict; identical events replay.

Placement requires four of five answers plus all critical answers. The same version is practice-only after its first submission. Placement and skipping do not complete lesson exercises.

Implementation caveat: current-release evidence filtering and placement-derived objective evidence must be verified before claiming all mastery semantics complete.
